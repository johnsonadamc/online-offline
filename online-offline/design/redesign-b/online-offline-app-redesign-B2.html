Principles

What makes B′ intuitive

The rules behind the changes, in priority order.

  1. One next stepThe dashboard answers "what should I do?" before "what do I have?". Everything else is a menu.
  2. Recognize, don't readIcons and color identify type; the only words are names. Status is a glyph.
  3. Decide inline, onceInvites get Join / Pass right where they appear. No detour to a detail page for a yes/no.
  4. Show the object you're buildingCurators are assembling a printed issue. The page meter makes 20 slots physical and turns selection into filling a book.
  5. Neon is earnedColor at rest is a dot. Glow only on the primary action and on what you just changed.
  6. Price is a consequence, not a statIt updates in the footer as you select. Never repeated on cards.
